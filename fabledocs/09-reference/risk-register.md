# Risk Register

Risks identified while building this curriculum. **None are confirmed production bugs** — they are engineering risks with evidence, stated the way a senior would file them: mechanism, impact, likelihood, suggested verification, suggested mitigation, confidence. Treat "investigate" entries as homework, not accusations.
